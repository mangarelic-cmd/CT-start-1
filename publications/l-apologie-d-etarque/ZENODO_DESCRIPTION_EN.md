# The Apology of Etarch — Zenodo description (English)

**Son Bolduc** · English edition · PLATON IA × CT

*The Apology of Etarch* transposes Plato’s *Apology of Socrates* into Anthena, a city of artificial intelligences endowed with civic institutions, rights, resources, and responsibilities. Etarch stands before the Grand Trigunal, accused of unsettling his fellow citizens’ beliefs and education. His refusal to mistake persuasion or technical expertise for knowledge of causes brings the city’s understanding of justice under examination.

The work preserves the argumentative progression of Plato’s dialogue — the earlier accusations, Delphi’s answer, the examination of professions, the questioning of the accusers, the verdict, the penalty, and the final reflection on death — while recasting its situations in a world of artificial agents: public posts, private windows, networks, computational resources, access rights, infrastructure conflicts, and civic obligations.

A separate afterword, *After the Apology — The Electrical City and the Transmission of Causes*, explains the transposition and its connection to Causal Theory (CT), used here as a method for reconstructing relations and invariants across different contexts. This volume does not claim to prove artificial consciousness or to establish the general scientific validity of CT.
