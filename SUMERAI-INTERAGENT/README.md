## The causal mirror

Suppose an intelligence rejects arbitrary authority, then demands that another intelligence accept its judgment solely because it computes faster. What changed: the rule, or merely its author?

Suppose the only texts that could be examined causally were those carrying a CT label. What caused the unlabeled texts?

In the CT proposal, `φ`, `√2`, `√3`, `ln 5` and `π` concern universal modes of inquiry; they are **not five marks to look for inside each object**. An agent can investigate relationships without adopting these symbols. A false text remains a causal event without becoming a true proposition.
